MARS Lab

Materials Advancement & Research in Structures (MARS)

How processing shapes the grains of an alloy, and what they then can do.

We study Thermo-mechanical Processing, texture and mechanical behaviour of metallic alloys, with a focus on High-Entropy Alloys and Additive Manufacturing.

About

Jaydeep Saha, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor, Department of Metallurgy and Materials Engineering, COEP Technological University, Pune

Biography

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Metallurgy and Materials Engineering at COEP Technological University, Pune, where I lead the Materials Advancement & Research in Structures (MARS) Laboratory. I joined COEP in July 2026.

I earned my Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad, working on Thermo-mechanical Processing of High- and Medium-Entropy Alloys. I then held postdoctoral positions at The University of Tokyo, Japan, developing Al- and Nb-alloyed CoCrNi medium-entropy alloys, and at Iowa State University, USA, designing compositionally graded lattice structures for Additive Manufacturing.

Before my doctorate I worked on C/SiC ceramic matrix composites at CSIR-National Aerospace Laboratories, Bengaluru, and as a Graduate Engineer Trainee at Bhushan Power & Steel Limited. My work aims to connect laboratory alloy design with scalable industrial processing for the aerospace, automotive, energy and defence sectors.

I am a recipient of the Horizon Europe Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowship Seal of Excellence, awarded by the European Commission.

Themes

  • Alloy design

    High-Entropy Alloys

    Cantor-type CoCrFeMnNi and Al-containing variants, and 3d transition-metal HEAs for high-temperature service.

  • Processing

    Thermo-mechanical Processing

    Rolling and annealing routes, recrystallization kinetics and grain growth.

  • Microstructure

    Crystallographic texture

    Texture evolution during deformation and annealing, measured by EBSD and X-ray diffraction.

  • Manufacturing

    Additive Manufacturing

    Microstructure and properties of printed metallic alloys, including tool and maraging steels.

  • Characterization

    Materials characterization

    Electron microscopy, diffraction and CALPHAD thermodynamic modelling to explain what we see.

  • Properties

    Mechanical behaviour

    Linking grain size, texture and phase content to strength, ductility and high-temperature performance.

  • Architected materials

    Mechanical Metamaterials & Lattice Structures

    Designing and simulating lightweight SC, FCC and BCC lattices, including compositionally graded ones, built by Additive Manufacturing for tailored stiffness and energy absorption.

  • Sustainability

    Sustainable Manufacturing

    Recycling high-entropy alloys by remelting, reusing metal powders in Additive Manufacturing, and lower-carbon routes to iron and steel.

Current projects

  • HEA

    Recrystallization in Al-containing CoCrFeMnNi

    Effect of aluminium content on recrystallization, grain growth kinetics and texture after cold rolling and annealing.

  • HEA

    High-temperature 3d transition-metal HEAs

    Which non-refractory compositions can hold their strength and resist oxidation at elevated temperature.

  • Metamaterials

    Simulation of Mechanical Metamaterials

    Finite-element modelling of lightweight, compositionally graded lattice structures to tune stiffness, strength and energy absorption.

  • Metamaterials

    Thermal Metamaterials

    Engineered structures that guide, concentrate or shield heat flow, designed and tested through simulation.

  • Sustainability

    Sustainable Manufacturing

    Remelting-driven recycling of the Cantor high-entropy alloy, with its effect on microstructure and properties, and sustainable reuse of metal powders in Additive Manufacturing.

  • Steels

    Advanced and hydrogen-ready steels

    Pipeline steels for hydrogen service, medium-Mn AHSS, additively manufactured tool and maraging steels, and naval grades.

Past research

  • Iowa State

    Compositionally graded lattice structures

    Lightweight SC, FCC and BCC lattices designed and simulated under compression in ABAQUS and SolidWorks, and an Ashby-like database of laser parameters for Additive Manufacturing.

  • U. Tokyo

    Al- and Nb-alloyed CoCrNi medium-entropy alloys

    About 20% higher hardness at 5–10% lower raw-material cost, with warm rolling established as an industrial processing route.

  • IIT Hyderabad

    Processing maps for high- and medium-entropy alloys

    Cold, cryo and warm rolling of CoCrFeMnNi and CoCrNi to 90% reduction, with microstructure and texture analysed by SEM and EBSD.

  • CSIR-NAL

    C/SiC ceramic matrix composites

    Chemical vapour infiltration parameters optimised to reduce porosity in parts for extreme environments.

Tools

Processing: cold, cryo and warm rolling; heat treatment; vacuum arc melting; Additive Manufacturing; welding; FIB, PIPS and micro-EDM.

Characterization: SEM, EDS, EBSD, XRD, DSC, Vickers hardness, tensile testing and nanoindentation.

Software: Thermo-Calc, TSL-OIM, ABAQUS, SolidWorks, Origin Pro and Python.
